### What it is

Retellings of traditional folk tales for adults who cannot sleep. One complete tale per part, told beginning to end in plain, warm prose. Every tale is public-domain folklore retold in our own words — never a translator's wording. Nothing startles: no cliffhangers, no raised voice, no moral tacked on the end. Say where a tale comes from and how old it is, so the listener knows what they are hearing. This is never children's content and is never written or read in the register of a children's book.

### Title grammar

Name the tradition and say it is for sleep, the way a listener actually searches. Under 60 characters. Prefer "folk tales" or "old tales" to "fairy tales", which reads as children's content and pulls the made-for-kids flag. No clickbait punctuation, no numbers unless the video really is a numbered collection.

### Visual mood

Quiet Japanese landscape and interiors: mist on mountains, bamboo, snow on pines, paper lanterns, rain on a wooden porch, still water, moonlight, ink-wash texture, cranes, koi, temple roofs. Held 20-40 seconds, cross-faded, low contrast, no faces.
